## Simple plan — Puppy Raffle first
1. Create free account on codehawks.cyfrin.io
2. Go to First Flights, AI Judged tab, click Start on Puppy Raffle. Clock starts: 7 days.
3. Read the code together: what is it supposed to do? Who can enter, who can win, who gets the money?
4. Look for the simple bugs first: can someone enter twice, can someone win without paying, can the owner take all the money, can the draw be cheated?
5. Write each bug in plain words: what happens, who loses what, how to show it.
6. Submit on the site, get instant feedback, learn what you missed.
